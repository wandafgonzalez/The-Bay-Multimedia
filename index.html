Our Story

ROOTED IN THE BAY.
BUILT ON CREATIVITY.

The Bay Multimedia is an independent creative media company rooted in Richmond, California, serving artists, businesses, creatives, and brands throughout the Bay Area.

Founded by Wanda Gonzalez — an R&B singer, songwriter, photographer, videographer, and creative — the company was built from a passion for music, visuals, technology, and bringing ideas to life.

We believe every vision deserves to be captured, every story deserves to be told, and every brand deserves to stand out.
